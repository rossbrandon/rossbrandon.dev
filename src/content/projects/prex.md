## What Is It?

A multi-tenant, AI-powered, product recommendations service for Adobe Commerce merchants to suggest related and high-performing products based upon the patterns of their shoppers. Allows for the deployment and management of product recommendation units on our customer's storefronts to increase shopper engagement and conversion rates.

## What Did I Do?

- Lead engineer for the Adobe Commerce Product Recommendations platform from its inception through its launch and evolution.
- Led development team in delivering a high-performance, scalable recommender system including:
  - An enterprise scale microservices architecture.
  - Multi-tenant shopper behavioral data collection, aggregation and modeling.
  - Machine learning pipeline for calculating co-occurrence indicators, statistics, and embeddings for vector search.
  - Ultra-low latency indexings and querying for sub-200ms storefront response times.
