## What Is It?

Spendy, both v1 (PHP/Laravel) and v2 (TypeScript/React), were my custom budget and expense tracking apps since out of the both solutions like Mint and YNAB did not fit my customization needs. As with all of my side projects, they serve as a way for me to learn and experiment with new technologies rather than be something novel. During the creation of the PHP version, I had just joined Magento (a PHP shop) and was looking for a project to jump start my experience building a real full-stack application in PHP-land. The following React/Node version was created from my real-world usage of the first version and the desire to vastly improve my frontend skillset.

For in-depth information on each version, dive into the blog posts below!
