## What Is It?

Adobe Commerce Optimizer, affectionately known as ACO, is an extreme-scale e-commerce platform that has been designed from the ground up to support and modernize large, complex multi-store and multi-region e-commerce needs without having to completely re-platform. Leveraging [AEM Edge Delivery Services (EDS)](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/experience-manager-cloud-service/content/edge-delivery/overview), ACO can increase traffic growth and converstion rates with fast, engaging, and intelligently personalized shopper experiences.

## What Did I Do?

- Systems Design & Architecture.
- Led engineering team responsible for core services, machine learning pipeline, and AI personalization features.
- Led developer experience intiatives to streamline customer onboarding and integrations, including:
  - Implemented API-first strategy for administrative APIs implementing federated GraphQL approach to optimize customer developer experience.
  - Designed self-updating SDKs for catalog data synchronization between upstream integrations and Commerce Optimizer. Currently released languages: [Typescript](https://github.com/adobe-commerce/aco-ts-sdk), [Java](https://github.com/adobe-commerce/aco-java-sdk), and [Python](https://github.com/adobe-commerce/aco-python-sdk).
- Designed the [Salesforce Commerce Cloud Starter Kit](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/commerce-learn/adobe-commerce-optimizer/sfcc-starter-kit/overview) setting the example for a key integration pattern between third-party e-commerce platforms and Commerce Optimizer.
- Implemented pre-rendering solution (ACO catalog -> EDS storefronts) for PDP and PLP pages to improve SEO and performance.
