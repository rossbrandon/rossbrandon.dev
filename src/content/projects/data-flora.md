## What Is It?

A proof-of-concept site demoing the visualization of data lineage. Tracing, auditing, and reconciling data as it flows between a complex network of microservices is a critical component of any serious scale production architecture. While this site is a simplified example with fake data, it serves as a reference for the engineering and design concepts involved.
